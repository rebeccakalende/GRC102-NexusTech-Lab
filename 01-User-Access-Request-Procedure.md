# NexusTech Solutions User Access Request Procedure

**Version:** 1.0  
**Procedure Owner:** Information Security Manager  
**Approved By:** Marcus Vance, CEO  
**Effective Date:** 18 September 2026  
**Review Date:** 18 September 2027  

## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines the process for requesting, approving, provisioning and reviewing user access to NexusTech Solutions systems and information.

It ensures that access is authorised, appropriate to the user's role and based on the principle of least privilege.

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to employees, contractors, consultants, interns and other authorised users who require access to NexusTech systems, applications, networks or information.

It applies to new access requests, changes to existing access and removal of access.

## 3. Prerequisites

Before access is provisioned, the following must be available:

- A valid business need for the requested access.
- The user's identity and employment or contractual status must be verified.
- The requested system or application must be clearly identified.
- The user's role and required level of access must be specified.
- Approval from the user's manager must be obtained.
- Where required, approval from the relevant system or data owner must be obtained.

## 4. Access Request Intake

All access requests must be submitted through the approved IT service desk or ticketing workflow.

Informal requests through personal conversations, chat messages or unapproved email requests must not be used as the basis for provisioning access.

The request must contain:

- User's name and department.
- System or application requested.
- Required access level or role.
- Business justification.
- Manager approval.
- System or data owner approval where applicable.

## 5. Verification and Approval

The IT Helpdesk or authorised IT staff must review the request to confirm that:

1. The request was submitted through the approved workflow.
2. The user's identity and employment status are valid.
3. Required approvals are present.
4. The requested access is appropriate for the user's role.
5. The requested permissions follow the principle of least privilege.

Incomplete or unauthorised requests must be returned to the requester for clarification or approval before access is provisioned.

## 6. Account and Access Provisioning

Once the request has been properly approved:

1. The authorised IT staff member creates or updates the user's account.
2. The user is assigned the appropriate system role or permission level.
3. Access is limited to the minimum permissions required to perform the user's duties.
4. Appropriate security controls are applied to the account.
5. The access provided is recorded in the service desk ticket.

IT staff must not provide access beyond the level approved in the request.

## 7. User and Manager Notification

After access has been provisioned:

- The user must be informed that access has been granted.
- The manager must be notified where appropriate.
- The systems or applications provided must be clearly identified.
- Any relevant security responsibilities must be communicated to the user.

Credentials or authentication information must be communicated through approved secure channels.

## 8. Evidence and Ticket Closure

The IT Helpdesk must record sufficient evidence in the service desk ticket, including:

- Original access request.
- Required approvals.
- Date of approval.
- Date access was provisioned.
- Access level or role assigned.
- Person who provisioned the access.
- Confirmation that the request was completed.

The ticket may only be closed after the required evidence has been captured.

## 9. Emergency Access Requests

Emergency access may be granted where immediate access is necessary to protect business operations, systems or information.

Emergency access must:

- Have a documented business justification.
- Be approved by an authorised manager or system owner where practicable.
- Be limited to the minimum required access.
- Be recorded in the service desk system.
- Be reviewed after the emergency has been resolved.
- Be removed when it is no longer required.

## 10. Exceptions

Any exception to this procedure must be documented and approved by the Information Security Manager and relevant system or data owner.

The exception must include the reason, affected system, approved access level, duration and any required security controls.

## 11. Record Retention and Audit Trail

Access requests, approvals, provisioning records and related evidence must be retained according to NexusTech's approved record retention requirements.

Records must be available for security reviews, audits and investigations where required.

## 12. Access Review

User access should be reviewed periodically by the appropriate system or data owners to confirm that permissions remain appropriate for the user's current role.

Access that is no longer required must be removed promptly.

## 13. Roles and Responsibilities

### IT Helpdesk / IT Team

- Receive and process access requests.
- Verify required approvals.
- Provision approved access.
- Maintain accurate records and audit trails.
- Remove or modify access when authorised.

### Managers

- Confirm the user's business need for access.
- Approve or reject access requests.
- Notify IT when access is no longer required.

### System/Data Owners

- Approve access to systems or sensitive information under their responsibility.
- Confirm appropriate permission levels.
- Participate in access reviews.

### Information Security

- Provide security requirements and oversight.
- Monitor compliance with access control requirements.
- Support investigations and periodic reviews.

### Users

- Request only the access required for their duties.
- Protect their accounts and authentication information.
- Report inappropriate or unnecessary access.

## 14. Procedure Review

This procedure will be reviewed at least annually or earlier when there are significant changes to systems, business operations, security requirements or identified access control risks.
