# NexusTech Solutions Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Policy Owner:** Information Security Manager  
**Approved By:** Marcus Vance, CEO  
**Effective Date:** 18 September 2026  
**Review Date:** 18 September 2027  

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the acceptable and unacceptable use of NexusTech Solutions' information systems, devices, networks, applications and other technology resources.

The purpose is to protect company information, client data and technology resources from misuse, unauthorised access, malware and other security risks.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all NexusTech employees, contractors, consultants, interns and other authorised users who access company information systems or technology resources.

It applies to company-owned devices, corporate networks, applications, cloud services, information systems and data.

## 3. Acceptable Use

Users must:

- Use NexusTech technology resources primarily for authorised business purposes.
- Protect company devices, accounts, passwords and other authentication information.
- Use only software and applications that are authorised by NexusTech.
- Follow information security policies and procedures when accessing company systems.
- Protect confidential company and client information from unauthorised access or disclosure.
- Report suspected security incidents, lost devices or unauthorised access to the IT Helpdesk or Information Security team immediately.
- Use corporate systems responsibly and in a manner that does not compromise security or business operations.

## 4. Prohibited Activities

Users must not:

- Install or download unauthorised software on company devices.
- Attempt to bypass security controls or gain unauthorised access to systems or data.
- Share passwords, authentication codes or account credentials with other people.
- Access, copy, modify or disclose information without appropriate authorisation.
- Use company systems to conduct illegal activities.
- Introduce malware, malicious software or other harmful content into company systems.
- Use company resources to harass, threaten or discriminate against others.
- Connect unauthorised devices to corporate systems where this creates a security risk.
- Store or transfer confidential company or client information using unauthorised personal cloud storage services.
- Use company resources in a way that negatively affects system availability, performance or security.

## 5. Reasonable Personal Use

Limited personal use of NexusTech technology resources may be permitted provided that it:

- Does not interfere with work responsibilities or business operations.
- Does not create security, legal or compliance risks.
- Does not involve prohibited activities.
- Does not result in excessive use of company resources.
- Does not involve storing or sharing confidential company or client information through personal services.

NexusTech may restrict personal use where it creates a security or operational risk.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

### 6.1 Users

Users are responsible for:

- Following this policy and related security requirements.
- Protecting their accounts, devices and information.
- Reporting security incidents and suspected violations.
- Completing required security awareness training.

### 6.2 IT and Information Security

IT and Information Security are responsible for:

- Implementing and maintaining appropriate technical security controls.
- Supporting users with security-related issues.
- Monitoring technology resources where authorised.
- Investigating reported security incidents and policy violations.
- Providing security awareness and guidance.

### 6.3 Managers

Managers are responsible for:

- Ensuring their team members understand and follow this policy.
- Approving access to systems where required.
- Reporting repeated or serious policy violations.
- Supporting security awareness activities.

### 6.4 Human Resources

Human Resources is responsible for:

- Supporting enforcement of this policy.
- Coordinating disciplinary action where appropriate.
- Ensuring policy requirements are communicated to employees.
- Supporting the acknowledgement process.

## 7. Compliance and Enforcement

All users are required to comply with this policy.

Violations may result in actions appropriate to the nature and severity of the violation, including removal of access privileges, disciplinary action, termination of employment or contractual action, and referral to relevant authorities where required.

NexusTech may review relevant system activity when investigating suspected violations, subject to applicable requirements.

## 8. Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy must be formally requested and approved by the Information Security Manager and the appropriate business owner.

Approved exceptions must have a documented business justification, defined duration and appropriate risk controls.

## 9. Policy Acknowledgement

All users within the scope of this policy must acknowledge that they have read, understood and agree to comply with the Acceptable Use Policy.

## 10. Approval and Review

This policy is owned by the Information Security Manager and approved by the CEO.

The policy will be reviewed at least annually and may be reviewed earlier following significant changes to technology, business operations, security risks, regulatory requirements or a security incident.
