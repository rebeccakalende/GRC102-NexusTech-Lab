# NexusTech Solutions Communication and Training Plan

**Related Documents:** Acceptable Use Policy and User Access Request Procedure  
**Plan Owner:** Information Security Manager  
**Approved By:** Marcus Vance, CEO  
**Effective Date:** 18 September 2026  

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this plan is to ensure that NexusTech employees understand the new Acceptable Use Policy and User Access Request Procedure and know how to apply them in their daily work.

The plan focuses on awareness, training, acknowledgement and follow-up to ensure that the approved requirements are adopted across the organisation.

## 2. Target Audiences and Key Messages

| Target Audience | Key Message |
|---|---|
| General Employees | Use NexusTech systems responsibly, protect company and client information, and follow the Acceptable Use Policy. |
| IT Staff | All user access must follow the approved request, approval, provisioning and documentation process. |
| Management | Managers are responsible for approving appropriate access and ensuring their teams understand and follow the security requirements. |

## 3. Communication Channels

The following channels will be used:

- Company-wide announcement
- Email communication
- All-hands meeting
- Company intranet
- Mandatory security awareness training
- Manager and team briefings
- Follow-up reminders

## 4. Rollout Timeline

| Stage | Activity | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Company-wide announcement of the approved policies | Week 1 |
| 2 | Distribute policy documents through email and intranet | Week 1 |
| 3 | Conduct mandatory security awareness training | Week 2 |
| 4 | Conduct IT staff briefing on the access request procedure | Week 2 |
| 5 | Employees complete acknowledgement/attestation | Week 2 |
| 6 | Follow-up reminders and address questions | Week 3 |
| 7 | Review adoption and identify areas requiring additional training | Week 4 |

## 5. Acknowledgement and Attestation

All employees and other users within the scope of the policies must confirm that they have:

- Received the relevant policy information.
- Read and understood the requirements.
- Agreed to comply with the Acceptable Use Policy.
- Understood the approved process for requesting system access.

Acknowledgements will be recorded and retained as evidence of completion.

## 6. Training Content

Training will focus on practical examples of expected behaviour, including:

- Acceptable and prohibited use of company technology.
- Protection of company and client information.
- Reporting suspected security incidents.
- Safe use of company devices and networks.
- The correct process for requesting system access.
- Importance of manager and system owner approvals.
- Least-privilege access.
- Risks associated with unauthorised access and software.

## 7. Measuring Effectiveness

The effectiveness of the communication and training plan will be measured using:

### 7.1 Training Completion Rate

The percentage of users who complete the mandatory security awareness training will be tracked.

### 7.2 Policy Acknowledgement Rate

The percentage of users who complete the required policy acknowledgement will be monitored.

### 7.3 Access Request Compliance

IT will review access requests to determine whether requests are being submitted through the approved workflow and contain the required approvals.

### 7.4 Follow-up Questions and Incidents

Repeated questions, policy misunderstandings and relevant security incidents will be reviewed to identify areas requiring additional communication or training.

## 8. Non-Compliance and Escalation

Where users fail to complete required training or acknowledgement, managers will be notified and follow-up communication will be provided.

Repeated non-compliance or continued misunderstanding of the requirements will be escalated to the relevant manager, Information Security and Human Resources for appropriate action.

Additional training may be provided where misunderstanding is identified as the cause of non-compliance.

## 9. Responsibilities

### Information Security

- Coordinate the communication and training programme.
- Provide training materials and security guidance.
- Track completion and acknowledgement.
- Review effectiveness and recommend improvements.

### IT

- Support technical questions relating to access and systems.
- Monitor compliance with the access request process.
- Provide relevant technical guidance.

### Managers

- Encourage participation within their teams.
- Follow up on incomplete training or acknowledgement.
- Support understanding and adoption of the requirements.

### Human Resources

- Support employee communication and enforcement.
- Assist with escalation of repeated non-compliance.
- Maintain relevant employee acknowledgement records.

## 10. Review

The communication and training plan will be reviewed after implementation to determine whether the target audiences understand and are following the requirements.

Improvements will be made where training gaps, repeated misunderstandings or compliance issues are identified.
